OUR STORY

Where it all began

I still don't know where to begin, because this friendship was never something I planned; it grew, one little moment at a time. I remember seeing you in class, sitting with a friend on the third bench from the back. We introduced ourselves, started talking, and before long we were choosing the same seats. Somehow, we moved from the back of the classroom to the front bench, and a simple hello became one of the most important friendships in my life. We talked about everything and shared parts of ourselves we didn't share with everyone. People came into our lives and moved on, as they do, but our friendship kept growing stronger—like a wall we could lean on. Not because life was always easy, but because we kept showing up for each other. Sometimes I wonder how I got so lucky in friendship. Maybe I really am one of the luckiest people in the world to have you in my life. Thank you for standing beside me in my lowest moments and sharing in my happiest ones. I never take your presence or your friendship for granted. Wherever life takes us next, I hope we keep choosing this friendship, supporting each other's dreams, and making many more memories together.

Always grateful for you ♡
