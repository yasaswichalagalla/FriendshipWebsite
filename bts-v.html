THEIR JOURNEY

Seven voices, one shared story

♡

BTS is a South Korean group of seven members—RM, Jin, SUGA, j-hope, Jimin, V, and Jung Kook. They debuted in 2013 and grew from performing together in Korea to sharing their music with fans around the world, known as ARMY. Their songs explore growing up, finding confidence, and caring for yourself and others.

V, whose name is Kim Taehyung, is one of the group's singers and performers. His deep, distinctive voice and expressive style make him stand out, while his solo album Layover shows another side of his artistry. BTS's story is about seven different personalities creating music together—and each member bringing something of their own to it.
