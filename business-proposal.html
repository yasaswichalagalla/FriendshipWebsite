THE STORY

A date that changes everything

Shin Ha-ri agrees to go on a blind date in place of her friend Jin Young-seo, hoping to help her friend avoid an unwanted match. The plan takes an unexpected turn when her date is Kang Tae-moo—the CEO of the company where Ha-ri works. A pretend identity, a determined CEO, and their everyday office life lead to plenty of funny misunderstandings and a sweet romance.

The story also follows Young-seo and Cha Sung-hoon, Tae-moo's trusted secretary, as they find their own way toward each other. It's a light series about friendship, mistaken first impressions, and letting people see the real you.
